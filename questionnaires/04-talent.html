PulseChain Network — Talent

Job: Supply + proof
Founder hunt: Who can ship, what stack they trust, and whether they will actually deploy.

Include: 00-contact-stub.md

Must

1. Most impressive thing you've built or accomplished

2. What can you offer others in this network right now?

3. Portfolio / GitHub / site / public proof links

Support

4. Roles that describe you

5. Able AND willing to deploy your strongest skill soon?

6. What do people typically come to you for help with?

High-ROI founder asks (add)

7. Favorite wallet / signing setup for real work (dev or power user)? Why?

8. Stack you ship with fastest (languages, frameworks, chains, tools) — free text short

9. Have you shipped anything that made money (or saved money) for someone else? What?

10. Hours/week you could put on a paid or equity collab in the next 30 days

11. Deal shapes you will take: cash · equity · revenue share · tokens · volunteer · intro-only

12. Coins/chains you actually build on or hold as a builder (names only) — where is muscle memory?

Why these ROI

  • Wallet/stack = hire/integrate without a discovery call.
  • Money-made proof > resume adjectives.
  • Hours + deal shape = match filter before wasting intros.
  • Builder chain set ≠ tourist coin list — different cohort.
