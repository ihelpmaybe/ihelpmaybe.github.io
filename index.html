About

Own your keys. Own your time.

I've always believed that people should have more control over their own lives, their money, and their future. That's what drew me to decentralization and eventually to HEX and PulseChain.

What I love about HEX is the idea of rewarding patience and thinking long-term. PulseChain gives me a place to build, experiment, and contribute to something I genuinely believe in.

This little corner of the internet is where I share what I'm working on. I enjoy building things, solving problems, and making crypto a little easier for everyone to use.

Everything here is built with curiosity, care, and a belief in what we can create together.
