About cryptocurrency

December 31, 2024

To donate cryptocurrency, you will first need some cryptocurrency.

Using a privacy-focused cryptocurrency, such as Monero (XMR) will protect the identity of the donor.

Fiat currency can be transformed to crypto at exchanges such as Kraken and then transferred to a self-custodial wallet like Cake Wallet for holding and subsequent transfer. It would not be wise to donate directly from an exchange.
